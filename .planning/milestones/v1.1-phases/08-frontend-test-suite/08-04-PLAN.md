<objective>
Cover the history event-card surface and close the folded crash bug: an `event_type` outside the
known union currently takes down the entire History route.

Purpose: `EVENT_BADGE[event.event_type]` is a total-looking lookup over a `Record` typed on a
three-literal union, but `GET /events` performs no client-side validation of the value it returns. An
unrecognized value makes the lookup yield undefined, and the next line dereferences it — throwing
during render, which the top-level error boundary catches by replacing the whole History route. One
bad row takes out every good row. Writing the first real test for this component is the natural place
to prove the fallback.

Per D-07 the fix lands as a RED-then-GREEN pair: a test commit that fails against current source, then
a minimal fix commit. The RED commit intentionally leaves the suite red for one commit, matching this
repo's Phase 02-03 and 03-03 precedent.

Output: `web/app/components/history/EventCard.test.tsx` and a guarded badge lookup in
`web/app/components/history/EventCard.tsx`.
</objective>
