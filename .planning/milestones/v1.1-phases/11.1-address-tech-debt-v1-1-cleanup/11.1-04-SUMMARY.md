## Accomplishments

- `TestRun_BootServesHealthThenGracefulShutdownOnCancel`'s health-poll loop rewritten as a `select` over `done`, a `10s` deadline, and a `50ms` ticker (labeled `break pollHealthy` to avoid the bare-`break`-only-exits-`select` trap); an early `run()` failure now reports its real cause instead of a generic health-timeout message (D-06, closes 09-REVIEW.md WR-01).
- `COVER_PKGS`'s exclusion filter changed from an unanchored `grep -v '/internal/db/sqlc'` to an anchored `grep -vE '(^|/)internal/db/sqlc$$'`, so a future same-prefix package (e.g. `internal/db/sqlcgen`) is never silently dropped from the coverage profile (D-07, closes 09-REVIEW.md WR-02).
- `docker-compose.yml`'s published port and `Makefile`'s `TEST_DATABASE_URL` reverted to `5432` (the uncommitted working-tree change was finalized and committed), with both files' comments rewritten to preserve the full 5433 story: Postgres's own default, the stray-container collision that forced the temporary remap, and confirmation that the stray container has since been shut down, making 5432 permanent (D-01, D-02).
