## Conflict resolution: empty-state branch priority

`10-PATTERNS.md` and `10-UI-SPEC.md` contradict each other on branch ordering. **`10-UI-SPEC.md` wins** — it is the approved design contract (`status: approved`) and explicitly states it locks this behavior.

- `10-PATTERNS.md` (line 237) says: check `isFiltered` first, then `hasOlderEvents`. **Do not follow this.**
- `10-UI-SPEC.md` (§ Trigger condition) says: check `hasOlderEvents` first, then `isFiltered`, then the plain fallback. **Follow this.**

Rationale, per the UI-SPEC: when a filtered view's every match happens to be retention-hidden, the retention explanation is the more specific and more useful truth. Everything else in `10-PATTERNS.md` remains valid.

## Corrections to 10-RESEARCH.md carried forward

- **`HasAnyEvent` uses no `LIMIT 1` inside its `EXISTS`** (`queries/events.sql:30-32`). RESEARCH.md's "Don't Hand-Roll" row suggests `EXISTS(SELECT 1 ... LIMIT 1)`; follow the existing file's idiom instead — `EXISTS` short-circuits on its own and the extra `LIMIT` would be noise inconsistent with its neighbour.
- **`recordingQuerier` (`internal/httpserver/events_test.go:189`) embeds a nil `sqlc.Querier`.** Adding a method to the generated `Querier` interface will NOT break compilation there — it will pass `go build` and then nil-panic at runtime the moment `Service.List` calls the new method through it. This is a trap RESEARCH.md does not mention.
