<objective>
The phase's tracer slice and the machinery that drives it: one real digest goes
from a due slot, through the outbox, into one Discord embed, and back out as one
atomic ack of the events plus both settings columns — then a ticker that asks the
question every five minutes and a composition root that starts and drains it.

Purpose: DGST-08 (all three types batched into one scheduled message), DGST-09
(zero-event slots send nothing), DGST-15 (slot record decides due, outbox decides
content) and the delivery half of DGST-05 all live on this path.
Output: `SendDigestIfDue` on `Notifier` and `Sink`, a first-cut digest body
builder, `DigestScheduler`, and the `cmd/server` wiring.

**Deliberately deferred to plan 22-03, not simplified away:** the digest body this
plan produces is real and production-quality for the happy path, but it renders
artist and title text unescaped, sorts by nothing in particular, and omits the
guest-feature host credit and the deluxe track-count suffix. Plan 22-03 supplies
all four before the phase closes. These are functionality gaps inside a settled
architecture — the builder's signature, its call site and the single-embed shape
do not change — which is exactly the kind of gap a tracer is allowed to carry.
Nothing here is a "v1" of a decision; every locked decision in CONTEXT.md is
delivered in full by the end of the phase.

**Architecture identity decision (assumption-delta check, D-14/D-16):** this phase
adds a second consumer of the same outbox and the same `notifying` lock. The
answer recorded here is *two callers, one shared lock and one outbox* —
add-alongside, not promote. `NotifyPending` and `SendDigestIfDue` stay two methods
on the one `Notifier`, sharing `n.q`, `n.sender`, `n.settingsReader`, `n.notifying`
and the existing `readSettings`/`listUnnotified`/`suppresses` helpers. No
`NotificationDispatcher` abstraction is introduced this phase; ADR-0002's
one-outbox-one-sender-lock invariant is what makes the sharing correct.
</objective>

`Start(ctx context.Context)` sets `s.runCtx, s.runCancel = context.WithCancel(ctx)`, logs one Info `digest scheduler starting` carrying the interval, creates `s.done = make(chan struct{})`, and launches one goroutine. That goroutine `defer close(s.done)`, runs `s.check()` **once immediately** before entering the loop (D-10 — this is a deliberate addition with no analog in this codebase; `robfig/cron`'s `@every` and `authgate.Manager.sweepLoop` both wait out the first interval, and this one must not, because it is what delivers criterion 3's restart catch-up), then loops on a `select` over the tick channel and `s.runCtx.Done()`, calling `s.check()` per tick and returning on Done. Stop the ticker with the seam's stop func on exit.

`check()` calls `s.sink.SendDigestIfDue(s.runCtx, s.logger, s.now())` and, on a non-nil error, logs one Error `digest due-check failed` carrying the error string and continues — a failing check never kills the loop.

`Stop(ctx context.Context) error` logs one Info `digest scheduler stopping`, then selects on `s.done` (return nil) versus `ctx.Done()` (call `s.runCancel` if non-nil, return `ctx.Err()`). This mirrors `Poller.Stop` exactly; the one divergence is that there is no `cron.Cron` to `.Stop()`, so the drain signal is this scheduler's own `done` channel rather than a context handed back by cron.

Guard `Stop` against being called before `Start` (nil `done`): return nil rather than blocking forever.

Log-level discipline (D-24): `digest scheduler starting`/`stopping` at Info, the per-tick outcome logging lives inside `SendDigestIfDue` (Task 1) and is not duplicated here, and a failed check at Error.
