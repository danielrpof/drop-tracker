<objective>
Wire the artist-art matcher into its two call sites: synchronously at add time for artists added from now on (D-06), and as a cooldown-bounded startup sweep over the watchlisted artists that don't yet have an image and haven't been attempted recently (D-06, D-07, D-12).

Purpose: the matcher from plan 13-02 is inert until something calls it. Without the startup sweep, every artist already on the watchlist keeps a placeholder forever, and bug #3 stays visible even though the matching logic exists. Without the add-time path, the bug returns for every artist added after the sweep. A `/grill-with-docs` challenge pass against the original design (13-CONTEXT.md's grilling-round decisions D-10–D-12) found the original wiring insufficient on three points folded in here: the sweep and add-time path had no coordination despite sharing the same rate-limited clients (D-10, Q1), a fail-closed artist would be re-attempted on every single restart forever (D-12, Q4), and there was no operational signal for whether matching is actually working in production (D-11, Q3).

Output: a bounded, resumable, cooldown-aware backfill sweep in `internal/artistart` that yields to interactive activity and reports its match rate; an optional matcher-and-gate dependency on `watchlist.Service` that leaves all existing constructor call sites untouched; and the `cmd/server/main.go` boot wiring that supplies both.
</objective>
