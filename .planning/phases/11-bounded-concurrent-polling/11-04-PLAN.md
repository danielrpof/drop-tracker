<research_correction>
11-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 7 asserts that all four todo-named `internal/notifier` tests "assert on real-time `time.Now()` deltas". Direct inspection of `internal/notifier/notifier_test.go` this planning session found only **two** timestamp-difference assertions in the entire file, at lines 380 and 479, belonging to `TestNotifyPending_BatchSpacingBetweenSends` (which the todo does not name) and `TestNotifyPending_SpacingAppliedEvenAfterFailedSend` (which it does).

The other three todo-named tests — `TestNotifyPending_SendFails_LeavesNotifiedAtNullAndRePicksUpNextPass`, `TestNotifyPending_CrossCycleRecoveryAfterOutage`, and `TestNotifyPending_BatchHonorsRetryAfterWithoutDroppingOtherRows` — record timestamps in one case but never compare them, and run with a spacing of one millisecond. They have no wall-clock assertion to be sensitive to, so their flakiness is attributable to the shared-database root cause of Pitfall 6, not to timing.

This plan is built on the corrected picture: Task 1 is expected to resolve three of the four named tests plus the `internal/poller` failure, and Task 2 addresses the two tests that genuinely do assert elapsed wall time. Task 3 verifies that split empirically rather than trusting it.
</research_correction>
