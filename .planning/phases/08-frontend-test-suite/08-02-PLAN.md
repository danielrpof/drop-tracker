<objective>
Cover the two watchlist behaviors ROADMAP success criterion 2 names by example, and establish the one
shared router-context helper success criterion 4 requires.

Purpose: these are the two behaviors the roadmap calls out verbatim — "the watchlist row's remove
control triggers the remove API call, and a preference toggle rolls back its optimistic state when
the call fails". The first cannot be proven from `WatchlistRow` in isolation: `WatchlistRow.tsx`
never imports `~/lib/api` and only calls its `onRemove` prop; the `removeWatchlist` call lives one
level up in the route. Rendering the route is therefore both required for the behavior and the
natural place to exercise the shared router stub.

Output: `web/app/lib/test/routeStub.tsx`, `web/app/routes/watchlist.test.tsx`,
`web/app/components/watchlist/PreferenceToggles.test.tsx`.
</objective>
