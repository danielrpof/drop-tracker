## Flagged assumptions

- **A3 (RESEARCH Open Question 3): stale `artist` param.** The UI-SPEC is silent on an `artist` id that's no longer on the
  watchlist. Per D-13's general rule, stale params are dropped silently. This plan drops it with replace once `HistoryFilters`' own
  `listWatchlist()` succeeds, and never on failure, mirroring the tag rule.
- **Copy outside the locked contract.** While the watchlist is loading or after it has failed, an active `artist` param makes the
  Artist trigger read `Loading artists…` or `Artist unavailable`. This mirrors the locked Tag copy, so the trigger never shows a silent
  `All artists` (RESEARCH Pitfall 10). The UI-SPEC has no row for this case. Flag it for `/gsd-ui-review`.
