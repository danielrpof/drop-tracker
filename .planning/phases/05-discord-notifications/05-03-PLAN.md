buried in the middle, real repeated cycles across a webhook outage and recovery, and a real
muted-preference detection run flowing all the way through the notifier's dequeue.

single-call test cannot fail on even if the underlying guard were subtly wrong. NTFY-04 is
also formally unclaimed by any prior plan's `requirements` field despite being fully
satisfied by Phase 4 -- this plan closes that gap with a regression test, not new logic.
