| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-24-SC | Tampering | npm installs | high | accept | No package is added; `web/package.json` and `web/pnpm-lock.yaml` are outside `files_modified`. |
</threat_model>
