<objective>
Close the one remaining re-verification gap: WR-08, which blocks TAG-06. `ManageTagsDialog.load()` applies every `listTags()`
settle through `setTags`, `setStatus` and `onLoaded`, and its `useEffect([open])` starts a new load on every open without
invalidating the previous one. Consider an open, a close, and a reopen. The reopen's load resolves, and the user deletes a tag.
Then the first open's response arrives late. It puts the deleted tag back in the dialog's list, and through `handleTagsLoaded`
back in the route vocabulary with status `loaded`. "+ tag" then offers it as an existing tag, and picking it silently re-creates
it (D-29 get-or-create). The same path can undo a rename or a merge. This falsifies the TAG-06 concurrency truth and the
prohibition that 24-11 declared. The verifier reproduced it deterministically.

The fix is a dialog-side generation ref, following the review's WR-08 sketch and the verifier's "missing" list:
- Each load captures a generation.
- A settle applies only while its generation is current.
- A successful delete, rename, or merge invalidates any in-flight load.

The verifier's sketch alone can strand the dialog on its loading skeleton. That happens when a rename succeeds while the
reopen's load is still in flight, because the bump drops the only pending load. So the invalidation also starts a fresh load
when one was in flight (Claude's discretion, rated reversible in Task 2).

The tracer drives the verifier's exact scenario end to end through the route. Task 2 adds the rename and merge variants and
the dialog-level edges. Task 3 rebuilds the embedded SPA and runs the phase gate.

Wave 1, with no dependencies: 24-10 and 24-11 are complete. Nothing in this plan touches the backend or the route component.

Purpose: the TAG-06 concurrency truth and the "never silently re-create a just-deleted tag" prohibition hold on every GET /tags
path, including Manage tags' own loads across a close and reopen.
Output: a generation guard plus invalidation in ManageTagsDialog, one tracer route test, two route variants, three dialog
tests, the refreshed embedded bundle, and a green whole-phase gate.
</objective>
