<assumption_delta_decision>
The assumption-delta detector fired on this phase (`{"detected": true}`, signal kind `chosen`,
term `configurable`). Re-reading the phase scope confirms a genuine, and more consequential,
assumption delta than the term the detector matched:

**The noun that changed:** "an artist drop-tracker knows about". Phase 2 (D-01) made the
MusicBrainz MBID the artist's identity — `artists.mbid` is `NOT NULL` and `deezer_id` is a nullable
attribute of that MusicBrainz-identified artist (Phase 2 D-02). This phase introduces a **second
catalog source that can produce an artist with no MBID at all**: a Deezer search hit is identified
only by a Deezer numeric id.

**Decision: `no-change`.** MBID stays the primary identity. Deezer results are returned as a
separate, source-tagged, informational list (D-02) and are not promoted to a co-equal identity.

**Rationale:** REQUIREMENTS.md lists dual-source reconciliation as explicitly out of scope, and
D-02 rejects cross-source matching for exactly the reason a promotion would require it. Promoting
to a source-agnostic artist identity now would mean a schema change (`mbid` nullable, a composite
or surrogate identity key) inside a phase whose CONTEXT.md states no new migration is expected.

**Accepted debt — surfaced, not hidden:** a Deezer-only search result has no MBID and therefore
**cannot be added to the watchlist** through the Phase 2 add path (`POST /watchlist` requires
`mbid`). `GET /search` will return Deezer artists the caller cannot act on. Phase 6 (UI-01) must
either disable "add" on Deezer-only results or the project must re-open the identity question then.
This is recorded here so Phase 6 inherits a known constraint rather than discovering it.

This checkpoint is advisory: it records and informs; it does not block the phase.
</assumption_delta_decision>
