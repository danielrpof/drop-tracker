uses `Select all visible`, and removes exactly the visible selection. Task 2 pins the tri-state and hidden-selection rules.

replaces 26-03's inline effective computation with the pure helpers every later bulk plan reuses.

Shift anchor, and route tests.

Locked inputs: D-09 (select-all next to the count), D-11 (derived, not trimmed; hidden selections inert; Clear and bulk
actions empty everything), and grill Q9 (unchecking keeps hidden selections). 26-UI-SPEC.md "Vendored-file edit", "Heading
row", and "Selection model" are verbatim contracts.
