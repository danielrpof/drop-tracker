checks two cards, confirms the count-stating dialog, and watches one request remove both cards and clear the selection.
Task 2 pins every copy and failure path of the remove flow.

architecture the later bulk plans extend: route-owned `selectedIds`, the effective selection derived from the visible

wiring, and tests.

## Flagged assumptions

- **[EDGE BULK-05 unclassified, unresolved]** The spec-less edge probe could not classify BULK-05, so the row stays
  `unresolved` and is not auto-resolved. A manual review produced truths across this plan and 26-04, 26-06, 26-08, 26-10,
  and 26-11:
  - empty: the bar is never mounted at zero, and removing the last artist shows `No artists yet` with focus on the `<h1>`;
  - concurrency: an id removed in another tab disappears on `refresh()`, and the derived selection ignores it (RESEARCH
    Pitfall F6), so no checkbox can point at it;
  - idempotency: a second action cannot reuse the cleared selection;
  - ordering: close, then clear, then focus (U1).
- **RESEARCH A1:** whether a label-synthesized click carries `shiftKey` is browser behavior. Capturing Shift on pointer-down
  avoids depending on it. This plan captures the flag in `WatchlistRow`; plan 26-04 consumes it for ranges and carries the
  real-browser backstop row to UAT.
